# 0033 — 1.0.0 is the Drupal 11 release; the Drupal 12 preview stays experimental

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-18, with the docs scope and the soak decided on 2026-10-26
- **Decided by:** the project owner, on the working session's proposal (an amendment of ADR 0000)

## Context

The roadmap tags 1.0.0 on or after Drupal 12's stable release, after milestones M7 to M10. Its cut line moves the
Drupal 12 preview out of 1.0's scope only if M6 is not merged by 2026-12-07.

On 2026-10-18 the state was:

- M0 to M5 are done. M6 slice 1 (the core-removals scanner and the pre-release opt-in) shipped as
  1.0.0-alpha.6, and M6 slice 2 (the Drupal 12 test-bed) was in review.
- Drupal 12 is at 12.0.0-beta2, with no release candidate.
- `main` still carries 0.9.2. Every Drupal 11 improvement of 1.0 is only on the integration branch: the frozen
  upgrade plan, the PHP-target stage, the core-removals scanner, and the deterministic findings and worklist.

Waiting for Drupal 12's stable release and for M7 to M10 would keep these improvements away from users for months.

## Decision

1. **1.0.0 ships Drupal 11 support as soon as it is stable.** This is the roadmap's cut line, applied by choice
   rather than because M6 slipped:
   - the Drupal 12 preview stays behind `DRUPILOT_ALLOW_PRERELEASE`;
   - it is documented as experimental (`docs/guides/d12-preview.md`);
   - it is not a release criterion of 1.0.
2. **A stabilization round replaces M7 to M10 before 1.0.0:**
   - the essential docs: `README.md` and `README_es.md` updated for 1.0, the upgrade page from 0.9
     (`docs/upgrading/0.9-to-1.0.md`) and the install page (`docs/getting-started/install.md`);
   - 1.0's new JSON artifacts (the upgrade plan, the findings, the worklist, the run manifest) are documented as
     experimental where they may still change. The 0.9 public surface stays frozen by `scripts/dev/contract.sh`;
   - a lab re-verification on a real module (autologout), and an upgrade of a project set up with 0.9.2;
   - `1.0.0-rc.1` on the integration branch, then a soak of 3 days.
3. **The owner gives the go.** Merging `integration/1.0.0` into `main` and tagging `v1.0.0` each need the owner's
   explicit confirmation.
4. **The deferred milestones become minor releases:**
   - 1.1 brings official Drupal 12 support after its stable release (the RC refresh, upgrade_status routing, the
     dependency-rewrite codemod, and a re-verification at GA);
   - 1.2 brings M7 (progress and resume) and M8 (the AI executor and replay);
   - 1.3 brings M9 (Drupal 8 and 9 sources, and `migrate.sh`) and M10 (the Drupal 7 track).

## Consequences

- This overrides the roadmap rule "1.0.0 is tagged on or after D12 GA".
- The rc criteria move with their milestones:
  - the Drupal 11 to 12 preview port moves to 1.1;
  - the Drupal 8 and 9 source ports move to 1.3;
  - the cold stability metrics (the M8 harness) move to 1.2.

  For 1.0 the criteria are the essential docs, CI green, the fixture matrix green, the end-to-end workflow green,
  no known data-loss bug, one real end-to-end Drupal 10 to 11 port, and a clean upgrade of a 0.9.2 project.
- The full docs migration moves to 1.0.x or 1.1: a README of 150 lines or fewer, the guides, and `FLOW.md` folded
  into the docs site.
- `migrate.sh` and the upgrade notice (roadmap 09-R7, 09-R13) move with M9. 1.0 upgrades a 0.9 project the way the
  alphas do, artifact by artifact:
  - the alias layer reads the renamed settings;
  - a 0.9 lock keeps 0.9's toolchain until it is refreshed;
  - generated configs are regenerated when their template marker is older;
  - a 0.9 assessment or test record shows as stale.

  The upgrade page documents this, and the lab checks it.
- OD-03: `1.0.0-rc.1` is cut before 2026-12-09, so the Drupal 10 end-of-life flip of the T=11 `auto` default ships
  in 1.0.1, as OD-03 provides.
- `DRUPILOT_FIXPOINT` still becomes `enforce` with `1.0.0-rc.1`, if the fixture matrix converges.
